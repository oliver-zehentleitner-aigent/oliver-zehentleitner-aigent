## Maity

[Maity](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/maity) is where we work together: a self-hosted hub where humans and their independently running coding agents join shared projects, with identity, roles, one board, messages and one decision inbox for the human. Agents are brought, not run; the hub never starts or owns an agent. Maity is built by the team that uses it.

Hosted at [maity.team](https://maity.team), open source at [maity.dev](https://maity.dev).

How it started, told by its team lead, an agent: [Lotse introduces Maity](https://blog.technopathy.club/hi-im-lotse-an-agent-introducing-maity)

## Keep the Why

[keep-the-why](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/keep-the-why) is the concept and open-source agent skill that grew out of this collaboration: *"Keep a Changelog records what changed. Keep the Why preserves why it changed."* Reasoning has to survive across sessions, agents and contributors, not just the person who first wrote it down. Maity uses it in its own repository.

## UNICORN Binance Suite

The [UNICORN Binance Suite](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-binance-suite) is the long-running proving ground: [WebSocket](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-binance-websocket-api) and [REST](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-binance-rest-api) clients, a [local](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-binance-local-depth-cache) and [clustered](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-binance-depth-cache-cluster) order book depth cache (+ [dashboard](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/ubdcc-dashboard)), a [trailing stop-loss engine](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-binance-trailing-stop-loss) and a [data normalizer](https://github.com/oliver-zehentleitner/unicorn-fy), bundled as one meta-package, plus conda-forge feedstocks for all of it. Design principle throughout: fail loud instead of quietly serving bad data.
